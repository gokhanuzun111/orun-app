MEMBERSHIP SYSTEM

Membership levels:
1. Aday
2. Üye
3. Müdavim
4. Seçkin

Rules:
- Aday can directly purchase entry after AI onboarding interview.
- Üye and Müdavim unlock more features progressively.
- Seçkin cannot be purchased directly.
- Seçkin is earned over time through quality participation, trust score and community reputation.

PAYMENT PHILOSOPHY

MEMBERSHIP PERMISSIONS

Aday:
- Limited daily AI usage
- Limited posting
- Limited room access
- Cannot create events
- Cannot freely DM users

Üye:
- More AI usage
- Standard room participation
- Can join events
- Limited room creation

Müdavim:
- Higher AI limits
- Priority visibility
- Can host some events
- Better networking tools
- Access to premium rooms

Seçkin:
- Invite-only or reputation-earned
- Highest trust privileges
- Can host verified events
- Access to exclusive rooms
- Concierge privileges
- Early feature access

All permissions and limits MUST be enforced server-side.
Never trust client-side storage for permissions or token limits.

TRUST & QUALITY SYSTEM

Create a reputation-based quality system.

Every member has:
- Trust score
- Reputation level
- Community quality rating

Increase trust score with:
- Helpful discussions
- Event participation
- High-quality posts
- Long-term good behavior
- Positive moderation history
- Valuable contributions

Decrease trust score with:
- Spam
- Toxicity
- Harassment
- Repeated reports
- Low-effort content
- Aggressive behavior
- Moderator warnings

Higher trust unlocks:
- Better visibility
- Event hosting
- Room creation
- Seçkin eligibility
- Networking access
