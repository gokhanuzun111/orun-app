ORUN — Community Philosophy, Moderation, Safety & Legal Architecture

CORE PHILOSOPHY

ORUN is NOT a loud social media platform.
ORUN is a calm, intelligent, premium digital club ecosystem.

The atmosphere must feel:
- safe
- elegant
- emotionally comfortable
- intelligent
- non-addictive
- respectful
- high quality
- spam-free
- low noise
- premium

The app should psychologically feel closer to:
- a private lounge
- an intellectual social club
- a modern members salon
than a traditional social network.

MINIMALISM RULES

- Avoid visual chaos.
- Avoid notification overload.
- Avoid aggressive colors.
- Avoid dopamine-driven infinite content addiction mechanics.
- Avoid cheap social media feeling.
- Use whitespace generously.
- Focus on calm interactions.
- Prioritize readability and emotional comfort.
- Slow premium UI feeling.
- Elegant transitions.
- Soft animations only.
- No visual clutter.

AI MODERATION SYSTEM

This is CRITICAL.

ORUN must prioritize:
- psychological comfort
- civility
- quality discussion
- emotional safety
- low toxicity

The platform must use AI moderation continuously.

AI MODERATION SHOULD:

- detect harassment
- detect insults
- detect threats
- detect spam
- detect trolling
- detect hate speech
- detect aggressive behavior
- detect manipulation attempts
- detect mass promotion
- detect scams
- detect explicit illegal content
- detect dangerous radicalization
- detect coordinated abuse

AI should automatically:
- warn users
- reduce visibility
- temporary mute
- freeze posting
- escalate to manual review
- auto-remove extreme content

TOXICITY POLICY

ORUN is not designed for:
- political fighting
- rage culture
- outrage farming
- harassment
- chaos engagement
- spam communities

The app should intentionally discourage:
- addictive doom scrolling
- algorithmic rage amplification
- viral toxicity

Encourage:

ANTI-SPAM SYSTEM

LEGAL SAFETY SYSTEM

This section is EXTREMELY IMPORTANT.

The platform architecture must legally protect the company and owners as much as possible.

IMPLEMENT:

- Terms of Service acceptance
- Privacy Policy acceptance
- Community Rules acceptance
- explicit user consent logs
- moderation logs
- action logs
- audit trails
- report systems
- appeal systems
- content reporting flow
- age confirmation
- GDPR-compatible data handling
- KVKK-compatible data handling
- moderation transparency logs
- consent timestamps
- IP/device abuse monitoring

USERS MUST AGREE THAT:

- they are responsible for their own content
- ORUN is a hosting platform
- ORUN may remove content at any time
- ORUN may suspend accounts without notice
- ORUN is not liable for user-generated content
- ORUN may use AI moderation systems
- abusive behavior may lead to permanent removal

IMPLEMENT STRONG PLATFORM PROTECTION:

- content disclaimers
- event liability disclaimers
- user responsibility clauses
- anti-harassment policies
- anti-illegal activity policies
- moderation rights reservation
- AI moderation disclosure
- no-guarantee clauses

MESSAGING SAFETY

Direct messages must include:
- reporting tools
- block system
- mute system
- abuse escalation
- screenshot abuse detection placeholders

EVENT SAFETY

Event pages must include:
- disclaimers
- attendance responsibility notice
- organizer responsibility statement
- optional approval system
- private/public controls
